What personal data we process

HypenSec Astra is a compliance readiness tool. To run it we process only the following personal data:

  • IP address of the device that sends a scan or report request, used for security and rate limiting.
  • Access key label: the name of the customer or person an access key was issued to.
  • Scan records: the website address you ask us to scan, the time, whether you confirmed you are authorised to test it, and the outcome and score.
  • Name and email address, only if you write to us.

Scan reports, screenshots and your access key are kept in your own browser's local storage, not on our servers. Report content passes through our server only while a scan or PDF is being produced. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on this service, and the fonts are hosted by us.

Why we use it

We use this data for the following purposes: to provide the scanning and report service you request; to keep a record that each scan was authorised by the person who requested it; to prevent abuse of the service and protect its security; and to answer your questions. We process it on the basis of your consent, which you give when you confirm authorisation and start a scan, or when you write to us.

Sharing and where data is stored

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with our hosting provider, which acts as our processor under a contract, and where the law requires it. The service is hosted in {{HOSTING_LOCATION}}.

How long we keep it

We retain scan records and IP addresses in our audit log for {{AUDIT_RETENTION}}, then erase them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Correspondence is kept until your question is resolved. Data in your browser stays until you clear it.

Your rights

  • Access: you may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Correction and erasure: you may request correction, completion, updating or erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent: you may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us; it is as easy to withdraw as it was to give. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and we will stop processing and erase your data unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Nomination: you may nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal: see below.

Children

This service is meant for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process the personal data of a child. If we learn that we have, we will erase it; we do not track, profile or target advertising at children. Because the service is not offered to children, we do not seek verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian; if that changes, we will obtain it before any processing.

Security and breaches

Connections are encrypted, access to the service needs an access key, and access to the audit log is restricted. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the affected people and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require.
