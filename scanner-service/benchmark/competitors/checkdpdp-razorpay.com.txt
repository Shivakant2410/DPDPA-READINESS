Consent banner

Section 6 — DPDP Act 2023 · 0 / 15 points

FAIL

WHAT WE FOUND

No consent banner detected in the rendered HTML on first load and no known CMP CDN is loaded. Under Rule 1, absence of evidence = evidence of absence.

EVIDENCE FROM YOUR SITE

No element with cookie/consent/gdpr markers found in the home page DOM and no CMP CDN in scripts.

HOW TO FIX

Deploy a DPDP-compliant CMP that shows a clear banner on first load with Accept All + Reject All at equal prominence and a granular categories view.

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

Banner appears within 1 second of first paint with no trackers fired yet
Accept All and Reject All are both same-size primary buttons
A "Manage preferences" link opens granular toggles for each tracker category
Choice persists across pages and is revocable from a footer link

Tracker gating

Section 6 — DPDP Act 2023 · 0 / 10 points

FAIL

WHAT WE FOUND

Trackers loaded ungated: Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, Segment. No Consent Mode default-denied snippet found.

EVIDENCE FROM YOUR SITE

Tracker domains in HTML: googletagmanager.com, clarity.ms, segment.(io|com)

HOW TO FIX

Gate every non-essential tracker behind explicit consent. Easiest path: Consent Mode v2 default-denied + a CMP that flips state on accept.

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

No tracker network requests until the user clicks Accept
After Reject, only first-party / essential requests fire
Consent state is logged with timestamp for audit

Consent Mode v2

Section 6 — DPDP Act 2023 · 10 / 10 points

PASS

WHAT WE FOUND

No Google Analytics or GTM loader detected — Consent Mode v2 is not required.

EVIDENCE FROM YOUR SITE

No reference to google-analytics.com / googletagmanager.com in home page HTML.

HOW TO FIX

Add a gtag("consent","default",{...all denied except security_storage, region:["IN"]}) snippet with strategy="beforeInteractive" so it runs before the GTM loader.

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

All six consent fields (analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, functionality_storage, personalization_storage) default to "denied"
security_storage stays "granted"
region:["IN"] ensures the gate applies to Indian visitors
CMP upgrades individual buckets to "granted" only after the user accepts
